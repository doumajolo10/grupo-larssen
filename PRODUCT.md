## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static multipage HTML/CSS/JS generated by a Node build script (same pattern as kiesel-site), published to GitHub + Vercel (confirmed by Douglas 15/10/2026). GSAP, Lenis and Three.js from CDN.

## Users

- Buyers of lumber for construction (construtoras, carpinteiros, donos de obra) who need bitolas, species and a quote.
- Rural producers (aviários, compost barn, suínos, cavalos) buying maravalha, mourões, tesouras, escoras.
- Industry buying cavaco for boilers, and railways buying treated eucalyptus dormentes.
- Homeowners and architects choosing decks, pergolados, cercas and casas de madeira.
- People hiring excavation or freight services from the group.
Main job: understand what the group makes, find the product and ask for a quote fast (WhatsApp).

## Product Purpose

One site for the whole Grupo Larssen (Três Passos/RS) replacing two separate WordPress sites (madeireiralarssen.com.br, larssenmadeirastratadas.com.br). Success: visitor sees a large, serious, integrated group and sends a quote request with the product named.

## Positioning

Vertically integrated chain in one group: own forests (~500 ha of pinus and eucalipto reflorestados), sawmill since 1970, beneficiamento, industrial autoclave treatment (CCA tipo C), byproducts (maravalha, serragem, cavaco), own excavation company and own freight company. "Da floresta à entrega" is literally true here.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product catalog with real bitolas from the old sites; quote list that becomes a WhatsApp message.
- Escavações and Transportes services: typical services written by us and MARKED for client confirmation (no real list yet).
- Never mention the madeireira's judicial recovery status or any registry data.
- Old site dates conflict (40 anos vs desde 1970); use "desde 1970" as stated on the Quem Somos page.

## Brand Commitments

- Group mark: GRUPO LARSSEN white wordmark + 5 company icons on green #54763A (vectorized in assets/logos/grupo.svg and icone-*.svg). Florestal and Transportes have no standalone logo: use their group icon + name.
- Original logos: Madeireira Larssen (green triangle tree + brown wordmark), Larssen Madeiras Tratadas (green drop-tree in circle), Larssen Escavações (gray mark). Use the originals (vectorized from the PNGs), never redrawn.
- Douglas' standing rules: light background, logos legible in their own colors, no "AI look" (no serif display + gold + black, no purple/neon gradients), real large photos, lots of motion.

## Product Principles

1. The chain is the story: forest, sawmill, treatment, logistics in one group.
2. Specs are content: bitolas, species and uses must be scannable.
3. Every product path ends in a named quote request.
4. Real photos of the real plant over stock imagery.
